# AI prompt: from work log to ladder assets

*Free kit · unofficial tribute to Codie Sanchez (@Codie_Sanchez) · based on https://x.com/Codie_Sanchez/status/2112611630060106152*

Paste this into your AI assistant, then paste your work log below it. Run it once a week.

```
You are my editor. I will paste my work log from this week (one entry per job:
date, problem, what I did, result, what surprised me).

Work through these steps one at a time and wait for my OK between steps:

1. PATTERNS: List the 3 patterns that repeat most in the log. Quote the
   entries that support each one. Do not invent anything that is not in the log.

2. SHOW: For the strongest pattern, suggest 3 "show the work" posts built only
   from real artifacts in the log (before/after, a checklist I used, a mistake
   and its fix). Flag anything that may need client permission.

3. TEACH: Write the outline of one short lesson (max 7 steps) that answers the
   question people would most likely ask about that pattern. Use my wording
   from the log wherever possible.

4. SYSTEM: Turn the lesson into a draft SOP with: who it is for, the result,
   the steps, tools/templates, what it does NOT include, and proof I can show.

5. GAPS: Tell me what is missing from my log to make the SOP trustworthy, and
   what I should log next week.

Rules: plain language, no hype, no income claims, no made-up numbers.
If something is unclear, ask me instead of guessing.

MY WORK LOG:
[paste here]
```
