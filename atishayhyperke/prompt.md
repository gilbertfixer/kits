# AI prompt: cold email reviewer (the pre-send check)

*Free kit · unofficial tribute to Atishay (@atishayhyperke) · based on https://x.com/atishayhyperke/status/2112906923058012235*

Paste this into any AI assistant, then paste your cold email (and, if you have them, your follow-ups).

```
Act as a strict cold email reviewer.

Step 1. Ask me, ONE AT A TIME:
  a) Who is the prospect (industry, role)?
  b) What number does that buyer already track (appointments, jobs,
     demos, calls)?
  c) What real proof do I have from that industry or a close one?
  d) What is the single action I want them to take?

Step 2. Score my email against these 14 checks. For each: PASS/FAIL
and a one-line reason.
  LENGTH  1. Around 70 words or fewer
          2. Close to 30 words if the offer is simple
  ASK     3. Exactly one call to action
          4. Ends on a specific, easy-to-answer question
  PROOF   5. From the prospect's industry (relevance over size)
          6. Uses the buyer's own metric
          7. Fits in one line or a PS
          8. (When testing) same proof, new structure
  EMAIL 1 9. Gives value: partial deliverable, diagnostic, resource
         10. Or states a clearly good offer plainly
         11. If it offers an audit, says what the audit produces
         12. Justifies the time "free" still costs
  FOLLOW 13. Each follow-up adds proof, context or a clearer step
         14. If I may have the wrong person, asks who the right one is

Step 3. Give the total score (/14) and rewrite the email so it passes.
Then write follow-up #1 and #2, each adding something new.

Rules: never invent proof, numbers or clients. If my proof is weak or
missing, say so and ask me for a real case.
```

**Rule of thumb:** 14 = send · 12–13 = quick fix · below 12 = rewrite.
